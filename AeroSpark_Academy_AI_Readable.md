Mentorship Structure
Short Regulatory Courses: One scheduled 20-minute individual mentorship session.
Technical Foundation Courses: Two scheduled 20-minute individual mentorship sessions.
Software and Simulation Courses: Two scheduled 30-minute individual mentorship sessions.
Bundle Learners: Four scheduled 30-minute individual mentorship sessions, including learning-path and project guidance.

Assessment and Certification Framework
The assessment method may be adapted to each course, but the following structure provides a consistent academy-wide standard.

| Assessment Component | Suggested Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly quizzes | 20% |
| Assignments or practical exercises | 25% |
| Final test | 25% |
| Final project or case study | 20% |
| Viva or project explanation | 10% |

Certificate Requirements
- Minimum 75% attendance.
- Completion of the required weekly quizzes.
- Submission of applicable assignments, practical work or final project.
- Minimum 60% combined score in the final evaluation.
- One reassessment opportunity may be provided when the minimum score is not achieved on the first attempt.
Recommended Certificate Details
- Student name and exact course title.
- Total learning hours and completion date.
- Final grade or performance category.
- Unique certificate number and verification link or QR code.
- Project title where applicable.
