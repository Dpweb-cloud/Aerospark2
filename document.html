2. What to Remove Across Academy Pages

  • Remove filler lessons created only to make the lesson count look bigger.
  • Do not count quizzes, PDFs, assignments, projects or certificates as lessons.
  • Avoid heavy university-level theory inside beginner foundation courses.
  • Avoid repeating the same sales message in every section of a course page.
  • Remove outdated portal screenshots or step-by-step regulatory instructions when the process has changed.

3. What to Add Across Academy Pages

  • Show: level · duration · lesson count · live classes · certificate · fee.
  • Use one module for each week. Each module below contains four real lesson topics.
  • Add one clear outcome for every module: what the learner should understand or be able to do.
  • Use diagrams, worked examples, simple exercises and aerospace/UAV cases wherever they help.
  • For regulation and quality courses, show a “content last reviewed” date and check the source before each batch.
  • Keep quizzes, assignments, downloadable material, doubt sessions and final projects separate from the lesson count.

Pricing note: These are recommended launch fees. Keep the same fee on the Academy card, course page and enrolment form. Review fees later based on batch demand and the level of instructor support.
